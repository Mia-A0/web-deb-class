Delicious Gravy Recipe

This gravy seems normal at first, but once it hits the plate, it totally takes over in the best way. It sneaks into every bite, making the whole meal taste way better than you expected. It’s basically the smooth, boss-level star of Thanksgiving without even trying.

Preperation Time

  • Total: Approximately 35 minutes
  • Preparation: 15 minutes
  • Cooking: 20 minutes

Ingredients

  • 4 cups of broth or stock
  • 10 tablespoons of butter
  • 2/3 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 tablespoon bouillon paste
  • salt and pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Warm The Broth/Stock: in a saucepan or in the microwave until steaming. Keep warm.
  2. Butter & Flour: Melt the butter in a medium saucepan over medium heat. Add the flour and cook, whisking constantly, until the roux is golden brown, 4 to 5 minutes. Whisk in the bouillon paste, if using.
  3. Whisking: Whisking constantly and quickly, slowly ladle in warm broth about 1/2 cup at a time. Combine fully before adding more. The mixture will look crumbly and may even look curdled. It's ok! Trust the process.
  4. Simmer:Whisking constantly and quickly, slowly ladle in warm broth about 1/2 cup at a time. Combine fully before adding more. The mixture will look crumbly and may even look curdled. It's ok! Trust the process.
  5. Serve: Serve immediately or keep warm to serve later. The gravy will thicken as it cools..
  6. Enjoy: Eat the delicious gravy, with additional salt and pepper if needed.
