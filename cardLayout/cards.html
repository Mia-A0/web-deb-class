The Detroit Lions are a professional American football team based in Detroit, Michigan, that compete in the National Football League (NFL). The franchise was established in 1930 as the Portsmouth Spartans and moved to Detroit in 1934, adopting the "Lions" name to symbolize being a king of the NFL. They play in the National Football Conference (NFC) and have won four NFL championships in 1935, 1952, 1953, and 1957.
