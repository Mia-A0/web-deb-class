Detriot Lions

The Detroit Lions are a professional American football team based in Detroit, Michigan, that compete in the National Football League (NFL). The franchise was established in 1930 as the Portsmouth Spartans and moved to Detroit in 1934, adopting the "Lions" name to symbolize being a king of the NFL. They play in the National Football Conference (NFC) and have won four NFL championships in 1935, 1952, 1953, and 1957

Chicago Bears

The Chicago Bears are a professional American football team founded in 1919 and are a charter member of the NFL. Based in Chicago, Illinois, they are known for their rich history, including nine NFL championships, and iconic players like Walter Payton. The team plays their home games at Soldier Field and are recognizable by their "bear head" logo. They are also known for their fierce rivalry with the Green Bay Packers.

Buffalo Bills

The Buffalo Bills are a professional American football team that competes in the NFL's American Football Conference (AFC) East division. Based in the Buffalo, New York area, the team was founded in 1960 as a charter member of the American Football League (AFL), winning two league championships in 1964 and 1965 before joining the NFL in 1970. The team is named after the legendary showman "Buffalo Bill" Cody and holds the distinction of appearing in a record four consecutive Super Bowls from 1991 to 1994, though they lost each time.

Los Angeles Chargers

The Los Angeles Chargers are a professional American football team, competing in the NFL's AFC West division and playing their home games at SoFi Stadium in Inglewood, California. The team, established in 1960, has a storied history, including winning the 1963 AFL Championship and reaching Super Bowl XXIX. Known for their distinctive "Bolt" logo and powder blue, gold, and white colors, the Chargers are an iconic franchise with a significant presence in the Los Angeles area.

Los Angeles Rams

The Los Angeles Rams are a professional American football team in the NFL, based in Inglewood, California, and competing in the NFC West. Founded in 1936 as the Cleveland Rams, the team has a history of relocation, including a return to Los Angeles in 2016 after a stint in St. Louis. Known for their blue, gold, and white colors, the Rams have won two Super Bowls (1999 and 2021) and two NFL Championships. They play home games at SoFi Stadium and are considered one of the NFL's most valuable franchises.
